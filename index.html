01 · About

Research, teaching and drug design.

My career combines academic teaching, computational research and early-phase drug discovery.

I am interested in using computation not merely to rank molecules, but to understand why they bind, how they change, and where a prediction can support an experimental decision.

My research interests include molecular dynamics, free-energy calculations, structure-based drug design, QSAR modelling, protein–protein interaction modulators and peptide design. My postdoctoral work in Nantes included large-scale in-silico screening against protein–protein interfaces and peptide-binder design validated through molecular simulations and free-energy calculations.

I have taught Organic and Medicinal Chemistry to pharmacy students and computational chemistry and drug design to master's and PhD students, with supervision of student computational-chemistry projects.
